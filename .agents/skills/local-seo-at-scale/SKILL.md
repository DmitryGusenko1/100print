# Local SEO at Scale

Use for city/service landing pages, location pages, structured data, internal linking, and programmatic SEO.

## Page strategy
Each indexable local page needs genuine local value: real service availability, turnaround, delivery/pickup details, portfolio/examples, equipment/capabilities, FAQs, contacts, and location-specific proof where available.

## Avoid
- swapping only city names in otherwise identical text;
- doorway pages with no unique user value;
- fake addresses or service claims;
- indexing empty combinations;
- conflicting canonical/hreflang/sitemap signals.

## Architecture
Define stable URLs, templates, metadata rules, schema.org entities, breadcrumbs, internal links, canonical rules, sitemap segmentation, and indexation gates.

## Measurement
Track leads/orders and conversion by landing-page class, not rankings alone. Prune or improve pages that attract no qualified demand.
