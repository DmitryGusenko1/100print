# Print Business Domain

Use for print-service taxonomy, production feasibility, terminology, order routing, and operational rules.

## Think in production stages
1. Product specification.
2. Artwork/prepress.
3. Material and format.
4. Print technology.
5. Finishing.
6. Quality control.
7. Packing/logistics.

## Key variables
Finished size, sheet/roll size, substrate, grammage/thickness, sides, colors, coverage, quantity, imposition, spoilage, setup, finishing operations, deadline, and delivery.

## Rules
- Do not quote a price from quantity alone.
- Separate technical feasibility from commercial desirability.
- Explicitly surface missing production parameters.
- Prefer standardization of product options to reduce estimating and production errors.
- Treat waste, setup time, reprints, and post-press constraints as real cost drivers.

When local machine capabilities are documented, use those facts instead of generic industry assumptions.
